Questions

Frequently asked questions

The short version — for anything else, just ask.

What size companies do you work with?

Startups and SMEs — from founders making their first hire to companies of around 200 people building a full HR function. Our support scales as you grow.

How are your fees structured?

Flat fees agreed up front, always. One-off projects (setting up contracts, a hiring process, a benefits scheme) are quoted as a fixed package; ongoing support runs as a monthly retainer sized to your needs. No hourly billing, no surprises.

Do you work in French and English?

Yes — we're fully bilingual. We draft French legal documents (contracts, handbooks, policies) and can run the whole relationship in English for international founders and teams.

You're remote-first — how does that work in practice?

Day to day, we work over video calls, email and your own HR tools — which keeps us responsive and keeps costs down. For workshops, interviews or sensitive sessions, we meet on site in Paris and across Île-de-France.

How quickly can we start?

The first consultation is free and we reply within one business day. Most engagements kick off within one to two weeks of that first call.
